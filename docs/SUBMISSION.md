## 90-second pitch structure

0–15s: The problem: existing building infrastructure has no unified, trustworthy reasoning and safe action loop.
15–40s: Show synthetic incident observation and local explanation.
40–60s: Demonstrate policy block for unsafe action plus authorized virtual alert.
60–75s: Execute virtual action and independent readback.
75–90s: Export auditable evidence; explain Meta-powered extension and product roadmap. Clearly distinguish the simulated demo from planned real deployment.
